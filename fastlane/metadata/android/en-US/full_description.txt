Signal for people who actually have friends.

Backchannel is a messaging app that talks to the Signal network. Your chats, calls and groups are the same ones your friends have in Signal, with the same end-to-end encryption. Backchannel only changes the app on your phone, to make big group chats and lots of friends easier to keep up with.

Backchannel is not made by Signal and is not affiliated with Signal Messenger LLC or the Signal Foundation. It is built on Molly, an open-source hardened build of Signal for Android.

WHAT BACKCHANNEL ADDS
Conversations
• Reply privately: answer someone's group message in a 1:1 chat, with their message quoted.
• Reply to one photo of a multi-photo message, with your own photos, videos, GIFs (or search for one), files or a sticker.
• Forward to as many chats as you like, with your own message on top and real @mentions in groups.
• Send one poll to many chats at once.
• Sticker replies, and double-tap a message to react.
• Starred messages.
• Remind me: long-press a message and pick when to be reminded about it.
• Note to Self works as a reminder: a new note stays unread until you open it.
• Keyword alerts: muted chats still notify you when someone uses words you pick.

On your phone, nothing leaves it
• Translate a message with your phone's offline translator.
• Transcribe voice messages on the phone (Android 13 and up).

Writing
• Markdown: *bold*, _italic_, ~strikethrough~, `monospace`, lists, indents, headings, quotes and code blocks, and everyone sees the formatting, Signal users too.
• Bold italic in one step from the format menu.
• Copying a styled message gives you styled text you can paste anywhere, even into a text message.
• Typed :) ;) :( turn into emoji, and one default emoji skin tone.

Photos
• Attach photos and videos straight from your own Immich server, or share from Immich to Backchannel.
• Attach through Google Photos or any photo app that lets you pick.
• The photo viewer stays inside the message you opened.

Contacts and privacy
• Link anyone to a phone contact, even if they hide their number, and it stays linked.
• Your own photo for anyone, even people not in your contacts.
• Linked contacts and your contact photos show on your linked computers too (Backchannel Web and Signal Desktop).
• Lock any chat behind your fingerprint.
• Optional: accept changed safety numbers without confirming each one (off by default).
• Passphrase is optional, and screenshots are allowed.
• Open Signal links in Backchannel.

EVERYTHING YOU EXPECT
Private messages, groups, voice and video calls, voice notes, photos and files, reactions, polls, stickers, disappearing messages, stories, scheduled messages and more.

MOVING OVER FROM SIGNAL
Make an on-device backup in Signal, restore it in Backchannel, then disable or uninstall Signal. Your chats come with you, and Backchannel takes over your number on that phone.

PRIVACY
Backchannel collects nothing. There are no ads and no analytics. Your messages go only to the Signal network, end-to-end encrypted. Notifications use Google push when it's available, with a direct connection as the fallback.
